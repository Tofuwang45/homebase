---
description: Use when someone asks what the week looks like, wants a weekly agenda, or asks who needs to be where.
---

1. Call `whats_today`, then `list_events` from now until the end of the coming Sunday.
2. Group events by day. For each day write the weekday and date, then each event as "time, title". All-day events come first.
3. Point out overlaps (two events at the same time) and early starts before 8:00 AM.
4. Keep it under 15 lines. Don't add events or advice nobody asked for.
